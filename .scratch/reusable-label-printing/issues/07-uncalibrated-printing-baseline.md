# Establish the uncalibrated printing baseline

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: 01, 02, 03, 04

## Question

When a controlled browser document uses the proposed sheet geometry without calibration, what repeatable offsets, scaling errors, variation across a sheet, and run-to-run feed variation appear on the available printers, sheets, operating systems, and browsers, and which observations are stable enough to guide a correction model?
