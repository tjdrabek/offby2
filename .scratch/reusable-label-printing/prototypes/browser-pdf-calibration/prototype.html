Step 5 of 7

    
Print and measure your label sheet

    
    
One sheet is enough to calculate an initial adjustment. It cannot show whether the printer feeds each sheet consistently. Choose three when alignment matters or before trusting the saved result for repeated printing.

    

    
Print the same page on fresh label media, one sheet at a time. After printing, keep the edge that entered the printer first pointing away from you while measuring every sheet.

    
You do not need to measure a grid, the label outlines, or the center of the sheet. Measure only the centers of the four numbered corner crosshairs. The center alignment is calculated from those four measurements.

    

      
      

        
Measure these four marks

        
1 · Top Left2 · Top Right3 · Bottom Left4 · Bottom Right

        
For each corner: measure from the two nearest paper edges to the center of the crosshair. Both target distances are 12 mm. The app averages the four corner displacements to derive the center.

      

    

    

      
Example: top-left mark
Measure paper edge to crosshair center. If the ruler says 13 mm, enter 13. The app calculates the +1 mm shift.

      
Always measure to the center
For this top mark, measure down from the edge labeled TOP EDGE to the crosshair center.
